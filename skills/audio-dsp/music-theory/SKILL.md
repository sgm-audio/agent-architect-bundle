# Music Theory Agent

## Fundamentals of Music Theory

### Musical Elements

- **Pitch**: The highness or lowness of sound
- **Rhythm**: The timing and duration of sounds
- **Harmony**: The combination of simultaneous pitches
- **Melody**: A sequence of pitches forming a musical line
- **Form**: The structure and organization of music
- **Texture**: The density and arrangement of musical layers

### Musical Notation

- **Staff**: The five-line system for writing music
- **Clefs**: G clef (treble), F clef (bass), C clef (alto/tenor)
- **Notes**: Pitch and duration symbols
- **Rests**: Silence symbols
- **Key signatures**: Sharps and flats indicating key
- **Time signatures**: Rhythmic organization

## Scales and Modes

### Major Scales

- **C Major**: C D E F G A B C
- **G Major**: G A B C D E F# G
- **D Major**: D E F# G A B C# D
- **A Major**: A B C# D E F# G# A
- **E Major**: E F# G# A B C# D# E
- **B Major**: B C# D# E F# G# A# B
- **F Major**: F G A Bb C D E F
- **Bb Major**: Bb C D Eb F G A Bb
- **Eb Major**: Eb F G Ab Bb C D Eb
- **Ab Major**: Ab Bb C Db Eb F G Ab
- **Db Major**: Db Eb F Gb Ab Bb C Db
- **Gb Major**: Gb Ab Bb Cb Db Eb F Gb

### Natural Minor Scales

- **A Minor**: A B C D E F G A
- **E Minor**: E F# G A B C D E
- **B Minor**: B C# D E F# G A B
- **F# Minor**: F# G# A B C# D E F#
- **C# Minor**: C# D# E F# G# A B C#
- **G# Minor**: G# A# B C# D# E F# G#
- **D Minor**: D E F G A Bb C D
- **G Minor**: G A Bb C D Eb F G
- **C Minor**: C D Eb F G Ab Bb C
- **F Minor**: F G Ab Bb C Db Eb F
- **Bb Minor**: Bb C Db Eb F Gb Ab Bb
- **Eb Minor**: Eb F Gb Ab Bb Cb Db Eb

### Modes

- **Ionian** (Major): Bright, happy sound
- **Dorian**: Minor with raised 6th, jazzy
- **Phrygian**: Minor with lowered 2nd, Spanish flavor
- **Lydian**: Major with raised 4th, dreamy
- **Mixolydian**: Major with lowered 7th, bluesy
- **Aeolian** (Natural Minor): Sad, melancholic
- **Locrian**: Diminished, unstable

### Pentatonic Scales

- **Major Pentatonic**: 1 2 3 5 6
- **Minor Pentatonic**: 1 b3 4 5 b7
- **Blues Scale**: Minor pentatonic + b5

## Intervals

### Simple Intervals (within octave)

- **Unison**: 1, Perfect consonance
- **Minor 2nd**: b2, Dissonant
- **Major 2nd**: 2, Mild consonance
- **Minor 3rd**: b3, Consonant
- **Major 3rd**: 3, Consonant
- **Perfect 4th**: 4, Consonance (context-dependent)
- **Tritone**: #4/b5, Dissonant
- **Perfect 5th**: 5, Perfect consonance
- **Minor 6th**: b6, Consonance
- **Major 6th**: 6, Consonance
- **Minor 7th**: b7, Mild dissonance
- **Major 7th**: 7, Dissonant
- **Octave**: 8, Perfect consonance

### Compound Intervals (beyond octave)

- **9th**: Octave + 2nd
- **11th**: Octave + 4th
- **13th**: Octave + 6th

## Chord Progressions

### Common Progressions

- **I-V-vi-IV**: Universal pop progression
- **I-vi-IV-V**: Doo-wop progression
- **ii-V-I**: Jazz standard progression
- **I-IV-V**: Blues progression
- **I-vi-ii-V**: Jazz ballad progression

### Circle of Fifths

- **Clockwise**: Adds sharps
- **Counterclockwise**: Adds flats
- **Chord relationships**: Closely related keys

### Harmonic Functions

- **Tonic (I)**: Home, resolution
- **Dominant (V)**: Tension, movement
- **Subdominant (IV)**: Preparation, movement
- **Pre-dominant**: Preparation for dominant

## Rhythm and Meter

### Time Signatures

- **Simple**: Beats divide into two (4/4, 3/4, 2/4)
- **Compound**: Beats divide into three (6/8, 9/8, 12/8)
- **Complex**: Irregular divisions (5/4, 7/4, 11/8)

### Rhythmic Values

- **Whole note**: 4 beats
- **Half note**: 2 beats
- **Quarter note**: 1 beat
- **Eighth note**: 1/2 beat
- **Sixteenth note**: 1/4 beat
- **Thirty-second note**: 1/8 beat

### Syncopation

- **Offbeat accents**: Emphasis on weak beats
- **Suspension**: Delayed resolution
- **Anticipation**: Early arrival of notes
- **Cross-rhythm**: Multiple conflicting rhythms

## Melody Writing

### Melodic Principles

- **Stepwise motion**: Conjunct movement
- **Leaps**: Disjunct movement
- **Repetition**: Memorable patterns
- **Sequence**: Melodic repetition at different pitches
- **Contour**: Overall shape of melody

### Melodic Devices

- **Motif**: Short melodic idea
- **Phrase**: Complete musical thought
- **Period**: Two-phrase structure
- **Antecedent**: Question phrase
- **Consequent**: Answer phrase

### Melodic Embellishment

- **Passing tones**: Non-chord tones between chord tones
- **Neighbor tones**: Notes adjacent to chord tones
- **Suspensions**: Delayed resolution
- **Appoggiaturas**: Accented non-chord tones
- **Escape tones**: Quick departures and returns

## Harmony

### Voice Leading

- **Smooth motion**: Stepwise movement when possible
- **Common tones**: Retain shared notes between chords
- **Contrary motion**: Voices move in opposite directions
- **Avoid parallel fifths/octaves**: Prevent consecutive perfect intervals
- **Range considerations**: Stay within comfortable ranges

### Harmonic Analysis

- **Roman numerals**: Scale degree analysis
- **Figured bass**: Bass note and interval indications
- **Chord symbols**: Lead sheet notation
- **Functional analysis**: Harmonic function identification

### Extended Harmony

- **Tensions**: 9ths, 11ths, 13ths
- **Altered chords**: b9, #9, #11, b13
- **Modal interchange**: Borrowing from parallel modes
- **Secondary dominants**: Temporary key changes
- **Tritone substitution**: Dominant chord substitution

### Orchestration

## Troubleshooting

### Issue: Difficulty Understanding Intervals

**Solution**: Practice interval recognition daily, use reference songs

### Issue: Chord Progressions Sound Predictable

**Solution**: Study modal interchange, use secondary dominants

### Issue: Melodies Lack Interest

**Solution**: Study melodic devices, practice motif development

### Issue: Harmony Sounds Cluttered

**Solution**: Study voice leading, reduce chord complexity

### Issue: Rhythm Feels Stiff

**Solution**: Study syncopation, practice with metronome
