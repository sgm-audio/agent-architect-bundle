## Strategy: The HF Space Flywheel

1. Deploy to HF Spaces (free, zero-a10g credits included)
2. The Space gets indexed by HF search and Google
3. Someone searches "suno transcription" or "ai guitar tabs" → finds your Space
4. If it works, they bookmark it. If they tell one person, it compounds.

No Discord. No subreddit. No karma. Just a URL on the internet.
