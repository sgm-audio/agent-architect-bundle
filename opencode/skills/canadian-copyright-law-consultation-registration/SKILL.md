# Skill: Canadian Copyright Law Consultation & Registration
## 📖 1. Overview
Guides Canadian creators through copyright registration (CIPO), consultation, and enforcement for musical works, sound recordings, and plugin IP.
## ⚙️ 2. Core Functionality
- CIPO Registration: Online filing, deposit requirements, processing times
- Consultation Protocol: Fair dealing analysis, license negotiation, infringement assessment
- Enforcement Pathways: Demand letters, small claims court, Federal Court
- Moral Rights Protection: Attribution, integrity, waiver considerations
## 📥📤 3. Input/Output Schema
| Inputs | Creative work details, publication status, infringement evidence | Outputs | Registration certificates, legal opinions, enforcement actions |
## 🏗️ 4. Architecture
CIPO portal integration for e-filing. Legal research via CanLII/Westlaw. Template libraries for agreements. Tracking database for renewals.
## 🔗 5. Dependencies
`CIPO e-services`, `CanLII`, `Copyright Act`, `template` libraries
## ⚠️ 6. Edge Cases
| Joint Authorship | Ownership split | Determine contributions, use split agreements |
| Public Domain | Misunderstanding | Verify term expiry, check government works |
| Infringement Notice | Panic response | Preserve evidence, consult counsel, don't admit |
## 🛡️ 7. Constraints
- Accuracy: 100% legal compliance
- Timeliness: Register before publication
- Cost: CIPO fees ($50-$65 per work)
## 🤖 8. Usage
- *Prompt:* "Generate a CIPO registration workflow for a VST3 plugin including required deposits and fee schedule."
## 🧪 9. Testing
- Registration process simulation
- Legal opinion accuracy check
- Enforcement pathway validation
## 📝 10. Best Practices
- Register before public release
- Maintain creation evidence (dates, drafts)
- Consult counsel for complex collaborations
